Diagrams Matter

Diagrams play a huge role in effective planning whether you are building a program, website, entity relationship, or network. They help keep the project on track while assisting the person or team by shifting their focus on what has been accomplished, what still needs done, and allow the full view of logic flow. While it is nice to rely on something you are familiar with, there are many options to choose from so feel free to explore beyond what is shown here. Below are some options; however, the focus here will be drawio because it is free, has an offline version, and can be integrated with Microsoft Word as long as you have administrative privileges. If you are using a student account this is likely not allowed which is a bummer but a nice option otherwise.
